Evidence guide

  1. Write the goal, prerequisites and a small implementation plan.
  2. Build a minimal working path; record the help used and your decisions.
  3. Test normal, empty, invalid and failure cases; keep the results and debugging notes.
  4. Rebuild a selected component unaided and explain its behavior and limitations.
  5. Attach a code commit or assessment link. Review the acceptance check with your assessor and choose the next learning gap.

Built, tested, rebuilt and explained are independent evidence dimensions. Checking a box records your judgment; it does not certify mastery. Evaluation and security begin early. Schema validation does not establish factual correctness.

Use bounded read-only tools before actions, evaluate retrieval before agents, and justify extra infrastructure. A private preview or beta can demonstrate deployment. Production requires human approval, operational checks, rollback/recovery and a maintenance owner.
